Bholavashi Promotional Video Export Guide
=========================================

Main file:
frontend_flutter/play_store_screenshots/promo-video.html

Video format:
- 1080 x 1920 vertical
- 25 seconds
- Suitable for Facebook/Meta app install ads, reels, shorts, and Play Store preview style clips.

How to preview:
1. Open promo-video.html in Chrome.
2. Keep the browser zoom at 100%.
3. The animation loops automatically.

How to export:
1. Click "Record 25s".
2. Wait until recording finishes.
3. Click "Download video".
4. It will download: bholavashi-promo-vertical.webm

Optional MP4 conversion:
If ffmpeg is installed, run:

ffmpeg -i bholavashi-promo-vertical.webm -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart bholavashi-promo-vertical.mp4

Suggested ad copy:
ভোলার সব সেবা এখন এক অ্যাপে।
ফুড ডেলিভারি, লোকাল সার্ভিস, পেমেন্ট, সাপোর্ট এবং লাইভ ট্র্যাকিং।
ভোলাবাসী by Sohoj IT.

Suggested music:
- Clean upbeat corporate background
- Low volume, no copyrighted track
- 25 seconds loop-friendly instrumental

Important:
- The video uses current generated app screenshots from output/*.png.
- If app UI changes later, regenerate/update those screenshots and the promo will automatically show the newer screens.
